Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information or any financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and allowing you to manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-broker services. We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, database) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding your data, please reach out via the contact information on our site and we will respond promptly.
